# Contacts

- `contact_duplicates_list`: List possible duplicate contacts
- `contact_duplicates_dismiss`: Mark two contacts as not duplicates
- `contacts_list`: List contacts with pagination, search and their company
- `contacts_create`: Create a contact
- `contacts_upsert`: Create or update a contact, matched by phone, then email
- `contact_consents_list`: List a contact's marketing consents
- `contact_consents_set`: Set a contact's marketing consent
- `contact_journey_funnels`: How far a contact went in each funnel
- `contact_journey_get`: Get a contact's journey
- `contact_touchpoints_list`: List a contact's marketing touches
- `contacts_get_by_id`: Get a contact by ID with channels
- `contacts_edit`: Edit a contact
- `contacts_delete`: Delete a contact
- `contact_merge_merge`: Merge another contact into this one
- `contact_merge_preview`: Compare two contacts before merging them
