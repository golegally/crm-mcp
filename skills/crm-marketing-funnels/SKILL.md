# Marketing funnels

- `journeys_list_funnels`: List funnels
- `journeys_create_funnel`: Add a funnel
- `journeys_update_funnel`: Change a funnel
- `journeys_remove_funnel`: Delete a funnel
- `journeys_people`: List the people at a funnel step
- `journeys_report`: Count a funnel's drop-off
