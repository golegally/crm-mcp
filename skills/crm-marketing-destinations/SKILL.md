# Marketing destinations

- `marketing_list`: List connected ad platforms
- `marketing_connect`: Connect an ad platform
- `marketing_disconnect`: Disconnect an ad platform
- `marketing_replace_rules`: Set an ad platform's conversion rules
- `campaigns_refresh`: Refresh an ad platform's spend
- `campaigns_set_schedule`: Turn the daily spend read on or off
