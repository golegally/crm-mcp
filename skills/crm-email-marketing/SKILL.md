# Email marketing

- `email_marketing_list_suppressions`: List addresses never mailed
- `email_marketing_add_suppression`: Add an address never mailed
- `email_marketing_remove_suppression`: Take an address off the list
- `email_marketing_list_newsletters`: List newsletters
- `email_marketing_create_newsletter`: Add a newsletter
- `email_marketing_get_newsletter`: Get a newsletter
- `email_marketing_edit_newsletter`: Change a draft newsletter
- `email_marketing_remove_newsletter`: Delete a draft newsletter
- `email_marketing_audience`: Who a newsletter would reach
- `email_marketing_cancel`: Stop a newsletter
- `email_marketing_send`: Send or schedule a newsletter
- `email_marketing_sends`: Who a newsletter went to
- `email_marketing_test`: Send a test
- `email_marketing_list_domains`: List sending domains
- `email_marketing_add_domain`: Add a sending domain
- `email_marketing_edit_domain`: Change a sending domain
- `email_marketing_remove_domain`: Remove a sending domain
- `email_marketing_check_domain`: Check a sending domain
