# GoLegally CRM

Contacts, companies, deals, tasks, notes and conversations in GoLegally CRM.

## How it works

This package connects your AI assistant to one hosted MCP server at https://golegally-crm.mcp.pretuned.ai/mcp. It runs nothing on your computer. Your requests and the data they return go between your assistant and that server, and you sign in before it can reach your account.
